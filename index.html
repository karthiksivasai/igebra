Questions

Everything a principal
needs to know.

What exactly is AI Ready School?

India's first complete K-12 AI ecosystem — a personalised AI companion for students, intelligent tools for teachers, hands-on AI labs, and secure on-premise infrastructure. Not a single product — an entire AI layer built for schools.

How is this different from other EdTech platforms?

Most platforms add AI to existing content. We started from a different question: what does it mean to truly learn in an age of AI? Every product is rooted in learning science — designed to make students think deeper, not think less. Our philosophy: Human First, AI Next.

How long does implementation take?

Typically 4–6 weeks — covering infrastructure setup, teacher training, curriculum integration, and student onboarding. A dedicated team guides every step; you're never left to figure it out alone.

Do you support on-premise deployment?

Yes. Our Matrix infrastructure keeps all student data within your campus with no dependency on external cloud servers — designed for schools with strict data sovereignty requirements.

Will AI replace teachers?

No — that's core to our design philosophy. AI Ready School amplifies great teaching, it doesn't automate it away. Think of it as an intelligent co-pilot that handles the repetitive so educators can focus on what only a human can do.

Is this safe for children?

Absolutely. All student-facing products are built with age-appropriate guardrails, responsible AI principles, and strict data privacy standards — students interact with AI in a structured, guided environment.

Still have questions?

Our school specialists are happy to walk you through the platform, address specific concerns, and design a pilot programme — no commitment required.
